# Presentation Plan

## Hook
(In collaboration with Joe, this hook was made)

Hook → Guess what percent of people suffer from social media addiction: 17%
Now, guess what percentage of young adults suffer from cognitive disability (difficulty concentrating or remembering): 9.7%


## Product
Product → I will create a video that showcases the features of my game, either using a video to capture the whole process or using GIFs to capture snippets of the essential gameplay.


## Process
Process → I will be using snippets of my code alongside gifs to explain how I created each little function of my game to create the whole thing. (For example → Showing what little code allows the circle to move on click and also a gif of the timer running with the code) I will also showcase how I utilized functions and variables to allow the game to be ran with a click of a button. During the presentation, I would also pose questions to explain my thought process while creating each feature of the game. Additionally, resources such as the blog and learning log can highlight specific challenges and things I learned along the way, and for me specifically, the use of videos and how I learned specific features of swift like


## Conclusion
Conclusion → This combination of games showcases the possibility of how we can utilize a coding language to efficiently and effectively complete a mindful goal. We will then wrap it up by explaining our emotions during the entire process and our final thoughts as we finished and navigated through everything. (Takeaway of to keep on researching a question or ask for help until you understand your problem fully. On top of that, “simple is sometimes better”, rather than trying to build something extravagant from the start, its better to start with a core feature and build around it so that you don’t get roadblocked easily and can work on your issue better.
