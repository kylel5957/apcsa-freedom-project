# Entry 1
##### 11/6/25

### Context
Now, as a senior, it's time for another freedom project. However, unlike the past two years, this time I can use any language without being tied to any. With the internet and social media becoming more and more prevalent, I want to make an app with Swift that is brain-stimulating and beneficial. Since Joe and I have the same tool, we will be working together to create this app. [Swift](https://www.swift.org/documentation/) will be our main tool and [Godot](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/) will be our backup.

### Swift

#### Pros
* Easy to read and debug
* Perfect for Apple devices
* Less maintenance because of automatic updates

#### Cons
* Not compatible with Android
* Smaller community due to nicheness

#### Decision
The decision to use Swift was made because of the versatility and modernity of this language. The documentation is extensive, and there are numerous in-depth guides available online on YouTube and on their website.

### Getting Started
It was rough at first, as to use Swift, you must download Xcode for macOS devices or Visual Studio Code for Windows. This was confusing at first. I began downloading random files, unsure if it would even work. I began at [Swift's download](https://www.swift.org/install/windows/) website and tried my best to follow the instructions. Eventually, after downloading the files and downloading vsCode, I created a file that contained Swift beginner code and began watching [tutorials](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcLMOTEDd8Y&list=PLMRqhzcHGw1b89DXHOVA77ozWXWmuBkWX&index=3).

### Engineer Design Process (EDP)
As only have just begun, we are still narrowing down our problem that we wish to tackle. So far, we are considering making some sort of brain-stimulating game to train the brain. We believe that this will be something to consider because of the prevalence of social media nowadays, though still on a screen, it will be beneficial as it would try to encourage the user to think instead of getting instant gratification.

### Skills 
This being the final Freedom Project, I have been developing my collaboration and organization skills. In our freedom projects, when working with a partner, collaboration and organization skills are a must because of the nature of this project. Without organization, it would take significantly longer to find everything and might even require you to relearn some part of your own code because of how messy your code and files are. And if I wish to finish this freedom project, collaboration in the means of sharing what I've learned and asking for help as we are working with the same language would be the most beneficial way to make progress.

### Summary 
With this being the final freedom project, I hope that Joe and I create something that reflects our time with coding, to create something we can happily look back to. I hope to use my previous years of experience in order to further my skills for the future.
